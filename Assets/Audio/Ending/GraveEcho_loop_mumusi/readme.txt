Grave Echo
Dark Crypt Loop – Diminished Ambient (72 BPM)

Description
Grave Echo is a slow, dark ambient loop built around diminished harmony, low synth textures, and deep timpani-style percussion. It is designed to function as background music that adds tension without overpowering dialogue, sound effects, or gameplay.

The structure is minimal and stable, making it easy to place in exploration or suspense scenes.

Tempo: 72 BPM
Seamless loop: Yes

Recommended Use

Dungeon exploration

Crypt or graveyard areas

Horror and suspense scenes

Boss pre-battle buildup

Dark title screens or menus
